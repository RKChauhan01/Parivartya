How to Play

1

Move & Rotate: Use your finger to drag blocks horizontally and tap to rotate them before they settle into place.

2

Clear Lines: Fill a complete horizontal row to clear it from the screen and earn points.

3

Stay Ahead: The speed increases as you clear more lines. Keep the stack from reaching the top to stay in the game!

🏆 Compete with Friends: Link your social media profile when saving your score. Every score you achieve is automatically synced, allowing you to climb the leaderboard and challenge your friends to beat your high score!
