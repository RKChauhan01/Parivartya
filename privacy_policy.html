When a website is requested, the hosting provider and systems used to deliver and secure it may automatically process limited technical information. Depending on the hosting configuration, this may include an IP address, request time, requested page, browser or device information, and security or error logs. These records are generally used for website delivery, troubleshooting, abuse prevention, and security.

I do not use this policy to claim that every technical log is collected directly by me. The information processed and the retention period can depend on the hosting provider and its configuration.

4. Cookies and tracking technologies

The Parivartya main website is not intentionally configured by me to collect cookie-related information or to use cookies for advertising, analytics, or cross-site tracking. I do not operate a cookie-based analytics or advertising programme on this main website.

Some hosting, security, embedded-content, or third-party services may technically use cookies or similar technologies independently. I do not control technologies set by external websites or services. If I add cookies or similar technologies to the main website in the future, I will update this policy and provide any notice or choice required by applicable law.

Your browser may provide controls for viewing, blocking, or deleting cookies. Blocking them may affect third-party features you choose to use.

5. Legal basis for processing

Where applicable law (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 of India, and, where relevant, the GDPR) requires a legal basis, I process personal information on one or more of the following bases:

  • Consent — where you have chosen to provide information (for example, by emailing me or submitting a form), or where you have otherwise given clear permission.
  • Legitimate interests — to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the website, prevent abuse, and respond to enquiries, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligation — to maintain records or disclose information where required by applicable law or a valid legal process.
  • Performance of a contract — where processing is necessary to provide a product or service you have requested.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

9. Data security and retention

I take reasonable steps appropriate to the nature of the information and the services used to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. No internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Subject to applicable law, I aim to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary. As an indicative guide:

  • Enquiry and support emails: retained for up to 24 months from the last relevant communication, unless a longer period is needed for an ongoing matter or legal requirement.
  • Security and error logs: retained according to the hosting provider's configuration, typically for a short rolling period (for example, up to 90 days), unless a longer period is needed to investigate an incident.
  • Records needed for legal obligations or disputes: retained for the period required by applicable law or until the matter is finally resolved.

Technical log retention is also affected by hosting-provider settings and applicable requirements. Information that is no longer needed should be deleted or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable law.
